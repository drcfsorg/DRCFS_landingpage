About Us

Who We Are

DRCFS — the Data Research Council For Students — is Nepal's largest and fastest-growing AI community. Founded in 2021, we are a student-led initiative dedicated to making artificial intelligence and data science education accessible to every student in Nepal.

We organize bootcamps, workshops, research programs, and community events that bridge the gap between academic learning and real-world AI applications. Our mission is to cultivate a generation of data-literate, research-driven students who will shape Nepal's technological future.

"From Pokhara to Chitwan — we bring AI education to every corner of Nepal."

Our Focus

What We Do

AI & ML Bootcamps

Intensive, hands-on bootcamps covering machine learning, deep learning, and practical AI applications for students at every level.

Data Research Programs

Structured research initiatives where students tackle real-world data problems, build portfolios, and contribute to open knowledge.

Community Events & Talks

Regular meetups, guest lectures, and panel discussions featuring industry experts, researchers, and student innovators.

Regional Outreach

Expanding AI education beyond Kathmandu — bringing bootcamps and workshops to Pokhara, Chitwan, and beyond.

Student-Led Learning

Peer-to-peer learning circles, study groups, and mentorship programs run entirely by students, for students.

Open Knowledge Sharing

Free resources, tutorials, datasets, and open-source projects shared with the broader Nepali tech community.
